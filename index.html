About

Most of my work happens in environments where mistakes are expensive — users are experts, workflows are deeply operational, and a poorly designed interface has real consequences.

I'm especially drawn to the tension between automation and control: how AI can accelerate decisions without removing human judgment from the equation.

Outside of work, I paint, build small internet products that have a point of view, and systematically taste and rate every cookie place in my area.
